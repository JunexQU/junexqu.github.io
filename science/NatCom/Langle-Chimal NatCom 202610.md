## [Langle-Chimal, O.D., Knoblauch, S. & González, M.C. The WorkReach model for urban work location choices through economic complexity and informality. Nat Commun](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-78293-3)

### **Objective:**
- Present WorkReach, a model designed to provide behavioral insights into urban work location choice 

### **Case:**
- Four urban areas

### **Methodology:**
- DCM/MNL

### **Data Source**
- Census
- Economic complexity index
  
### **Findings:**
- Workers consistently commute farther to economically sophisticated areas
