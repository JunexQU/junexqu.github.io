## [Gu, X., Beuster, L., Liu, X. et al. Global patterns of inequality in pedestrian shade provision. Nat Commun](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69190-w)

### **Objective:**
- Quantify the inequality in shade distribution across neighbourhoods and examine the associations with green/grey infrastructure and socioeconomic indicators

### **Case:**
- 9 global cities

### **Methodology:**
- Gini
- Regression

### **Data Source**
- Shadow (Canopy height maps)
  
### **Findings:**

- Lower-income and peripheral neighbourhoods tend to receive significantly less shade on sidewalks
