## [Huang, C., Liu, X., Zhang, S. et al. A universal scaling law of intra-urban inequality. Nat Commun](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73015-1)

### **Objective:**
- Analyse over 11,000 global urban centers using multisource satellite data to quantify inequalities in thermal exposure, green space and economic activity

### **Case:**
- Global cities

### **Methodology:**
- Gini
- Scaling law

### **Data Source**
- NDVI
- UCDB
- Nightlight
  
### **Findings:**

- A doubling of city population is associated with an approximately 8-9% higher inequality
