## [Zhu, L., Wang, F., Nielsen, C.P. et al. Optimized spatial planning offers a dual solution for managing urban heat and air pollution in humid subtropical climates](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73855-x)

### **Objective:**
- Develop an optimized spatial planning that consider the intricate interplay between urban heat and pollution

### **Case:**
- GBA, China

### **Methodology:**
- WRF-GC climate change model
- Optimization

### **Data Source**
- Emission data
  
### **Findings:**

- Developing medium-density urban and increasing urban green space can significantly reduce urban warming and pollution
