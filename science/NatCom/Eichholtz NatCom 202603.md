## [Eichholtz, P., Kok, N. & Sun, X. The impact of minimum energy performance standards on the commercial real estate market](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-70684-w)

### **Objective:**
- Employ a modified DID model to assess the causal impact of the label C obligation

### **Case:**
- Netherland

### **Methodology:**
- DID

### **Data Source**
- Energy label
- Real estate
  
### **Findings:**

- Offices meeting the label C requirement experienced a 21.8% increase in transaction prices after the policy's formal adoption
