## [Zhang, Y., Hong, Y., Gao, S. et al. Bidirectional yet asymmetric causality between urban systems and traffic dynamics in 30 cities worldwide. Nat Commun](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-71377-0)

### **Objective:**
- Propose a spatio-temporal causality framework that bridges correlation and causation by identifying bidirectional causal relationships between urban systems and traffic dynamics

### **Case:**
- Global cities

### **Methodology:**
- STCCM

### **Data Source**
- TomTom congestion
  
### **Findings:**

- Asymmetric bidirectional causality, with urban systems exterting stronger influences on traffic dynamics than reverse in most cities
