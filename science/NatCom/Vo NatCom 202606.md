## [Vo, K.D., Ham, S.W., Roy, M. et al. Uncovering latent urban mobility patterns via smart-card and survey data fusion. Nat Commun](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73445-x)

### **Objective:**
- Recovery time-dependent OD flows by integrating household travel survey and transit smart-card data

### **Case:**
- Seoul and Singapore

### **Methodology:**
- Adaptive representation learning
- Supervised learning on aggregated representations
- IPF

### **Data Source**
- Household travel survey
- OD
  
### **Findings:**

- Transit is most competitive for intermediate distances (11-16 km)
