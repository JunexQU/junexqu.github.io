## [Quispe-Torreblanca, E., De Neve, JE. & Brown, G.D.A. Social status and the relationship between income rank and well-being in 109 nations. Nat Commun](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69729-x)

### **Objective:**
- Compare deprivation and relative rank accounts of the income-SWB relationship

### **Case:**
- 109 countries

### **Methodology:**
- Relative rank indicator
- Moderator

### **Data Source**
- Gallup World Poll survey
  
### **Findings:**

- In 80% of countries, subjective well-being is more strongly associated with within-nation rank of income than with absolute income or relative income deprivation
