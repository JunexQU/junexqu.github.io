## [Xu, X., Ou, S., Peng, T. et al. The potential role of truck-hailing and operational efficiency improvement in China’s road freight decarbonization. Nat Commun](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-71160-1)

### **Objective:**
- Assess the potential carbon emission impacts of truck-hailing and operational efficiency improvements under multiple scenarios

### **Case:**
- China

### **Methodology:**
- Vehicle fleet model

### **Data Source**
- Trunk gps
  
### **Findings:**

- Enhancements in load factor and reductions in empty running could cut China's road freight emissions by 9-24% in the near term and 23-31% in the long term
