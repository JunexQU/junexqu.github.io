## [Peraza-Mues, G.G., Resendiz, E., Figueroa-Soriano, R. et al. Scaling and Population Loss in Mexican Urban Centres. Nat Commun](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-75780-5)

### **Objective:**
- Whether cities experiencing population decline tend to sprawl or become more compact

### **Case:**
- Mexican

### **Methodology:**
- Scaling equation
- Radial probability density function
- Urban expansion factor

### **Data Source**
- GHS-Pop
- WorldPop
  
### **Findings:**

- Distances from the city centre have increased by 28% on average, driven by population losses in central zones combined with growth in peripheral regions
