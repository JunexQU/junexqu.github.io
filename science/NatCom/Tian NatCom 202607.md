## [Tian, Z., Zhang, C., Li, W. et al. Structural inequality shapes the daily rhythm of stationarity and mobility. Nat Commun](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-75778-z)

### **Objective:**
- Examine how volatility in daily rhythms is associated with structural inequality

### **Case:**
- US

### **Methodology:**
- STASIS model with daily activity cycle

### **Data Source**
- SafeGraph
- Built environment from EPA
  
### **Findings:**

- Greater variability in activity timing among CBGs with higher proportions of Black residents
