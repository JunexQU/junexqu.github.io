## [Greene, J.H., Gidden, M.J., Brutschin, E. et al. Drivers of technology diffusion speed in countries. Nat Commun](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73563-6)

### **Objective:**
- Analyze trends in the speed of technology diffusion at the national level across a large, heterogenous set of technologies

### **Case:**
- Global countries

### **Methodology:**
- Regression

### **Data Source**
- HATCH technology adoption dataset
  
### **Findings:**

- Newer, simpler, more standardised, smaller, less materially intensive and shorter-lived technologies diffuse more rapidly
