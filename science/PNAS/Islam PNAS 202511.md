### **Objective:**
- Develop CHARGE-MAP to model the spatiotemporal distribution of charging demand by ABM and charging behaviour by optimizing charger location and capacity

### **Case:**
- Virginia counties

### **Methodology:**
- ABM + Optimization

### **Data Source**
- Synthetic population
- Charging station
- POI
- Power grid
