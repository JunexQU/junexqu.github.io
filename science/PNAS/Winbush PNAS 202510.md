### **Objective:**
- Investigate associations between social versus non-social applications and examined the direction of prediction by evaluating whether smartphone use showed a perspective

### **Case:**
- US

### **Methodology:**
- Regression

### **Data Source**
- 10099 participants

### **Findings:**

- Weak or null associations between smartphone use and mood
