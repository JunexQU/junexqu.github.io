## [Behrer, A.P., Bolotnyy, V. Understanding the migratory response to hurricanes and tropical storms in the USA. Nat Hum Behav](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-025-02281-8)

### **Objective:**
- Use data on the paths of all Atlantic basin hurricans and tropical storms between 1992 and 2017 to study the migratory response to these storms 

### **Case:**
- USA

### **Methodology:**
- Regression

### **Data Source**
- County-to-county migrant
- Storms from FEMA

### **Findings:**

- Total population-weighted exposure to storms increases
