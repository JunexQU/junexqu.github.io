## [Newman, D.B., Gordon, A.M. & Mendes, W.B. Income and education show distinct links to health and happiness in daily life. Nat Hum Behav](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-025-02264-9)

### **Objective:**
- Conduct an ecological momentary assessment (EMA) study in which we measured daily health indicators (heart rate and blood pressure) and well-being (positive emotions, stress and coping)

### **Case:**
- APP countries

### **Methodology:**
- OLS

### **Data Source**
- APP registration
- ACS

### **Findings:**

- Income and education are important determinants of one's health and well-being
