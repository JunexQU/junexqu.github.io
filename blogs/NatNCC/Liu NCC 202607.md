## [Liu, H., Bai, X. Intercity connectivity enhances urban mobility resilience to extreme rainfall. Nat. Clim. Chang](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41558-026-02713-x)

### **Objective:**
- Quantify how long-term, stable inter-city connectivity affects short-term human mobility resilience to extreme rainfall 

### **Case:**
- Chinese cities
  
### **Methodology:**
- Regression
- ML

### **Data Source**
- Baidu and Tencent Migration
- SSP

### **Findings:**

- Cities with higher network centrality and stronger ties to weathier cities exhibit higher resilience
