## [Tang, J., Lyu, W., Zhao, P. et al. Human mobility dynamics for cost-effective flood emergency resource allocation. Nat Sustain](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41893-026-01886-9)

### **Objective:**
- Develop an agent-based model to capture human dynamics and incorporate them into emergency resource allocation strategies and future projections

### **Case:**
- China's large cities

### **Methodology:**
- ABM
- Cost-benefit analysis

### **Data Source**
- WorldPop
- Building Rooftop
- Land cover
- Preceipitation
- DEM
- Mobile phone
- POI
- SSP

### **Findings:**
- 43% of cities face notable resource demand-supply mismatches
