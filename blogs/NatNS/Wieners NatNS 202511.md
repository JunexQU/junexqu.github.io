## [Wieners, C., Lamperti, F., Dosi, G. et al. Policies for rapid decarbonization with steady economic transition and employment creation. Nat Sustain](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41893-025-01683-w)

### **Objective:**
- Extend DSK macro-financial agent-based model to analyse the effects of a broad set of global decarbonization policies on production, employment and macro-financial stability from 2022 to 2160

### **Case:**
- Global

### **Methodology:**
- DSK agent-based model

### **Data Source**
- Economic data

### **Findings:**

- An ensemble of industrial regulations and public subsidies coupled with a mild carbon tax is the most promising policy toolkit to support a rapid and orderly transition
