## [Zhao, J., Mo, B., Caros, N.S. et al. Housing exchange framework to reduce carbon emissions from commuting. Nat Sustain](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41893-025-01658-x)

### **Objective:**
- Propose a household housing exchange framework that incorporates better information unlisted occupied and assess its potential environmental benefits

### **Case:**
- Beijing, Munich, Singapore

### **Methodology:**
- Utility-based matching algorithm

### **Data Source**
- Beijing travel survey in 2020 and 2021
- Munich: household housing and travel survey
- Singapore: Household interview travel survey

### **Findings:**

- Housing relocation can lead to substantial reductions in commuting distances
