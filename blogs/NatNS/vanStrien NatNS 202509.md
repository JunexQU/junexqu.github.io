### **Objective:**
- Perform a global assessment of the spatial extent of the ecological impact of extra-urban road traffic over the past several decades

### **Case:**
- Global

### **Methodology:**
- RF
- Kernel density

### **Data Source**
- Traffic volume


### **Findings:**

- The extent of the global terrestrial land influenced by moderate to very high extra-urban road traffic increased by 53% since 1975
