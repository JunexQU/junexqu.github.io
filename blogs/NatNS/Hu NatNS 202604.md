## [Hu, S., Santi, P., Benson, T. et al. Ubiquitous data-driven framework for traffic emission estimation and policy evaluation. Nat Sustain](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41893-026-01797-9)

### **Objective:**
- Propose a scalable mesoscopic framework that generates near-real-time, high resolution emission inventories without relying on comprehensive individual-level inputs

### **Case:**
- Manhatten, USA

### **Methodology:**
- Yolo
- MOVES: EPA emission model

### **Data Source**
- Traffic cameras
- Mobile phone OD

### **Findings:**

- The congestion pricing leads that 8 weeks after the implementation, traffic volumes declined by 10%, resulting in 16-22% drop in emissions
