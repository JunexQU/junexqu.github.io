## [Li, T., Wang, Z., Kyba, C.C.M. et al. Satellite imagery reveals increasing volatility in human night-time activity. Nature](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10260-w)

### **Objective:**
- Quantify the timing, intensity, type, and direction of ALAN changes for every 15-arc-second pixel across the primary inhabited landmasses of Earth

### **Case:**
- Global

### **Methodology:**
- VZA-COLD algorithm

### **Data Source**
- 1.16 million daily NASA Black Marble NTL images

### **Findings:**

- Nightlights of Earth are dynamic, characterized by frequent and coexisting brightening and dimming
