## [Sheng, H., He, C. & Hu, W. Intercity economic ties amplify natural disasters spillovers in China. Nat Cities](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44284-026-00399-y)

### **Objective:**
- Quantify spillover effects across geographical and economic networks

### **Case:**
- China

### **Methodology:**
- Spatial economic model
- Centrality indicator

### **Data Source**
- Firm records with investment information

### **Findings:**

- Neglecting economic linkages leads to an underestimation of total economic losses by 52.5% to 57.5%
