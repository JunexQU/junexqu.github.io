## [Chen, Z., Litvine, A. & Shaw-Taylor, L. Railways and urban scaling in England and Wales over 190 years of development. Nat Cities](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44284-025-00351-6)

### **Objective:**
- Analyse nearly two centuries of railway development and population change

### **Case:**
- England and Wales

### **Methodology:**
- Scaling law, Accessibility and Gini
- OLS

### **Data Source**
- Railway map and population

### **Findings:**

- Rail provision and accessibility became more proportionally aligned with population size
