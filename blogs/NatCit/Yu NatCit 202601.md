## [Yu, C., Yuan, Q., Goodchild, A. et al. Increasing nationwide disparities in road freight emissions across cities. Nat Cities](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44284-025-00368-x)

### **Objective:**
- Examine the temporal-spatial relationships between emission intensities and the distribution of minority and low-income populations and population density

### **Case:**
- US

### **Methodology:**
- Moran's I
- OLS

### **Data Source**
- Emission

### **Findings:**

- Cities near freight corridors or terminals face disproportionately high emissions relative to their freight demand
