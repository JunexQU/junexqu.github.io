## [Sevtsuk, A., Basu, R., Liu, L. et al. Spatial distribution of foot traffic in New York City and applications for urban planning. Nat Cities](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44284-025-00383-y)

### **Objective:**
- Examine the estimated distribution of foot traffic in different areas of the city and illustrate the model's potential as a baseline for targeted infrastructure investments and hazard analyses

### **Case:**
- New York 

### **Methodology:**
- Trip generation

### **Data Source**
- Mobility survey

### **Findings:**

- Many streets in the outer boroughs experience foot-traffic levels comparable with those in central Manhattan but remain undercategorized for pedestrain priority
