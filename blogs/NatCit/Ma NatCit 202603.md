## [Ma, Y., Qiu, M., Wang, Y. et al. Empirical evidence of air pollution reduction from electric vehicle usage across Chinese cities. Nat Cities](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44284-026-00395-2)

### **Objective:**
- Use a large-scale dataset on EV charging records to quantify the effects of actual use of EVs on urban air quality

### **Case:**
- China

### **Methodology:**
- Regression
- IV

### **Data Source**
- EV charging record
- Air quality

### **Findings:**

- A 10% increase in EV charging is linked to reductions in surface NO2 and PM2.5 concentrations by 0.08% and 0.04%, respectively
