## [Li, C., Yu, L., Ndugwa, R. et al. Mapping urban slums and their inequality in sub-Saharan Africa. Nat Cities](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44284-025-00276-0)

### **Objective:**
- Integrate geospatial data with household surveys to map slum prevalence and asset-based wealth inequalities

### **Case:**
- Grid, 32 countries

### **Methodology:**
- Wealth index

### **Data Source**
- Household Survey

### **Findings:**

- 54.6% of the urban population live in slums
