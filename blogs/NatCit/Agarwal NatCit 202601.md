## [Agarwal, S., Fan, Y., Qian, W. et al. Affordable public housing and intergenerational mobility. Nat Cities](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44284-025-00375-y)

### **Objective:**
- Investigate how affordable housing public housing alleviates their budget constraints

### **Case:**
- Singapore

### **Methodology:**
- Regression
- DID

### **Data Source**
- Residents survey
- Housing transaction
- Credit and debit card

### **Findings:**

- Children whose parents benefited from the affordable public housing program have a 9.5% higher likelihood surpassing their parents in terms of housing status
