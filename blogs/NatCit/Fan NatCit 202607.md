## [Fan, Z., Loo, B.P.Y., Duarte, F. et al. Hidden patterns of urban mixing across five global cities. Nat Cities](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44284-026-00477-1)

### **Objective:**
- Leverage large-scale travel surveys and reveal hidden patterns in social mixing

### **Case:**
- Five global cities
  
### **Methodology:**
- Regression

### **Data Source**
- Travel survey
- OSM

### **Findings:**

- Mobility shapes experienced social mixing more than sociodemographic characteristics, home environment and transit proximity
