### **Objective:**
- Investigate whether the probability distributions of climate variables follow a general scaling form

### **Case:**
- 142 Global cities
  
### **Methodology:**
- k-mean clustering 
- kernel density
- radial decay model

### **Data Source**
- Population, osm
- Temperature and PM2.5
