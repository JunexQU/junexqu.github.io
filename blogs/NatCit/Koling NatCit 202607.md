## [Koling, A., Michalek, J., Armanios, D. et al. Effects of Uber and Lyft on jobs, wages and GDP. Nat Cities](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44284-026-00478-0)

### **Objective:**
- Explore how Uber and Lyft launches affect employment, earnings and GDP

### **Case:**
- US
  
### **Methodology:**
- Event study
- DID

### **Data Source**
- ACS

### **Findings:**

- Uber and Lyft entry led to an increase in both regional GDP per capita and in seasonal, temporary or otherwise intermittent employment per working age population
