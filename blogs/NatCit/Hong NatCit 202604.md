## [Hong, Z., Li, Z., Zhong, S. et al. Decarbonizing emissions from last-mile deliveries in Chinese cities. Nat Cities](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44284-026-00423-1)

### **Objective:**
- Quantify the individual courier-level GHG emissions from 1.9 million delivery couriers and produce a fine-grained emission map

### **Case:**
- China

### **Methodology:**
- Mileage-based emission

### **Data Source**
- E-commerce order data
- Mobile phone
- AOI

### **Findings:**

- An 83.5% increase in orders from 2023 to 2024 resulted in only 31.3% additional emissions
